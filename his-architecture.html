Product architecture case study

Hospital Information System (HIS)

An offline-first healthcare management platform architecture covering patient care, hospital operations, billing, diagnostics and administration.

Status: product requirements, architecture and implementation roadmap defined; full deployment not yet claimed

Back to Projects

Hospital-wide scope

The design brings clinical and administrative departments into a consistent shared information system.

Patients, OPD & emergency

Patient identity and duplicate resolution, registration, appointments, visit workflows, consultation billing and emergency intake.

Inpatient care, nursing & OT

Admissions, transfers and discharges; beds and wards; nursing workflows; operating theatre and anaesthesia processes.

Diagnostics, pharmacy & inventory

Laboratory and radiology workflows, medication and inventory management, procurement, receiving and stock controls.

Finance & hospital administration

Receipts and corrections, supplier payments, finance/audits, employee administration, attendance, HR and payroll.

Technical architecture

Local-first infrastructure

A central on-premises server connected to browser clients over a hospital LAN, designed to support routine operation during internet outages.

Implementation status

This case study describes completed requirements and architectural planning for a full Hospital Information System. It does not claim that every module has been implemented, independently certified, or deployed in a live hospital.

Engineering focus

My contribution is in the system's product requirements, workflow design, data model and deployment architecture.

Identity and continuity

Stable patient identifiers, controlled duplicate resolution and merge processes, and consistent numbering across clinical and financial documents.

Operational safeguards

Authorization by role and context, auditable adjustments, resilience to retry/duplicate operations, and printable workflows for real hospital workstations.
